Project 60

Family & Friends Edition

Gameplay

Add players via the Manage Players panel. Generate (or select) a topic. Start the timer. The active player must speak for one minute without:

  • hesitation
  • deviation
  • repetition

Stop the clock when another player challenges the speaker.

For a correct challenge of hesitation, deviation, or repetition, the challenger continues the topic with the time remaining.

For an incorrect challenge, the speaker resumes.

When the minute is up, rotate to the next player.

Min. 3 players. One player (not the speaker) volunteers to start/stop the 1-minute timer.

Finer Points

Hesitation means noticeable ums, pauses, lip-smacks, etc.
Deviation means straying off topic (though brief tangents are allowable if the speaker returns to the topic).
Repetition means reuse of a word. Exceptions: repetition of words in the topic as well as common link words or short words such as "and", "a", and "the" is allowable.
Plural forms (e.g. cat and cats) and derivatives (e.g. bake, baking, and baked) are distinct words and may each be used once.

Scoring
  • One point for the player speaking at the one-minute mark
  • One point to the challenger for a correct challenge
  • One point to the speaker for an incorrect challenge
  • One bonus point to a speaker who completes an entire minute
